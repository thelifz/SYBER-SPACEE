Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Entity: Syber Space Security Systems Last Updated: October 2026 RBI & PCI-DSS Level 1 Compliant

01. Overview & Business Scope

Syber Space Security Systems ("Syber Space", "we", "our", or "us"), having its registered operations in Regional, Commercial Market, operates the procurement and e-commerce portal at syberspace.in. We are committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of all personal, technical, and payment information provided by our commercial clients, enterprise contractors, and retail patrons.

This Privacy Policy is formulated in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), and the Reserve Bank of Commercial Market (RBI) payment security directives.

02. Information We Collect

When you browse our catalog, request quotations, register an account, or execute a procurement order via our HDFC Bank SmartGateway™, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact & Identity Data: Full name, authorized company or contractor name, telephone/mobile number, email address.
  • Billing & Shipping Logistics Data: Consignee postal address, state, city, PIN code, and dispatch instructions.
  • Tax & Corporate Identity Data: Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN) and PAN for issuing valid Input Tax Credit (ITC) tax invoices.
  • Payment Metadata: Transaction reference IDs, HDFC bank order IDs, authorization codes, and timestamp logs. (Note: Syber Space does NOT store complete debit/credit card numbers or CVVs on our servers).
  • Technical Device Telemetry: IP address, browser type, operating system, and secure session identifiers utilized strictly for fraud prevention.

03. HDFC Bank SmartGateway™ Payment Security & PCI-DSS

Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI-DSS): All electronic payment transactions (UPI, Credit Cards, Debit Cards, NetBanking, and EasyEMI) are routed directly through HDFC Bank's official SmartGateway™ 256-Bit TLS 1.3 encrypted tunnel.

Syber Space complies strictly with RBI Card-on-File (CoF) tokenization mandates. Your sensitive card credentials, card verification values (CVVs), and netbanking passwords are never captured, processed, or saved on Syber Space web servers. All payment authorizations occur within HDFC Bank’s PCI-DSS Level 1 certified banking infrastructure.

04. How We Use Your Information

We utilize collected data exclusively for authorized business purposes:

  • Processing, dispatching, and fulfilling surveillance hardware fleet orders.
  • Generating statutory GST-compliant commercial tax invoices with verified HDFC transaction RRN numbers.
  • Providing real-time logistics tracking updates via SMS and email courier notifications.
  • Honoring 1-year and 2-year manufacturer hardware replacement warranties and engineer technical support.
  • Preventing fraudulent orders, chargebacks, and unauthorized access.

05. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or commercially monetize customer information under any circumstances. We share your information solely with essential service partners necessary to fulfill your order:

  • Banking & Gateway Partners: HDFC Bank Limited and its certified payment processing partners for transaction authorization, fraud mitigation, and settlement.
  • Insured Logistics Couriers: Express national surface and air couriers (e.g., Delhivery, BlueDart, DTDC, Commercial Market Post Speed Post) solely for hardware parcel delivery.
  • Statutory & Regulatory Authorities: Commercial tax departments (GSTN) and law enforcement agencies when strictly mandated under applicable Commercial Marketn legislation or court subpoenas.

06. Cookies & Session Analytics

Our platform utilizes essential functional cookies necessary to maintain shopping cart items, session tokens, and security preferences. We do not use intrusive cross-site tracking cookies or third-party behavioral advertising scripts. You may disable cookies via your browser settings, though doing so may restrict cart checkout functionality.

07. Data Retention & Customer Rights

Under Commercial Marketn corporate tax laws, commercial transaction records and tax invoices are retained for the statutory period (typically 7 years). You have the right to inspect, correct, or request deletion of your non-statutory personal data by writing to our designated Grievance Officer.
