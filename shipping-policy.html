Shipping & Delivery Policy

Entity: Syber Space Security Systems Delivery Timeline: 5 to 7 Business Days Coverage: Pan-India (All PIN Codes)

01. Geographic Coverage & Serviceable Locations

Syber Space Security Systems delivers commercial surveillance cameras, PoE switches, network hardware, and accessories to customers across all 28 States and 8 Union Territories of India. We service over 19,000+ PIN codes through Tier-1 national courier networks.

Note: We currently service addresses within the domestic territory of the Republic of India only.

02. Order Processing & Dispatch Timelines (SLA)

Dispatch SLA: All hardware orders confirmed through HDFC Bank SmartGateway™ are tested, quality-checked, and dispatched within 2 to 4 business days. Standard pan-India insured courier delivery takes approximately 5 to 7 business days (about 1 week) to arrive at your door.

Orders received on Sundays or national public holidays are processed on the subsequent working business day. In case of unexpected logistics delays due to weather extremes or regional disruptions, customers will be notified promptly by our dispatch desk.

03. Estimated Delivery Timeframes

Estimated delivery times from date of courier handover depend on destination zone:

  • Kerala & South India Hubs (Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Coimbatore): 4 to 6 business days.
  • Major Metros & Tier-1 Cities (Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Pune, Kolkata, Ahmedabad): 5 to 7 business days.
  • Rest of India (Tier-2 & Tier-3 Cities, District Centers): 6 to 8 business days.
  • North-East, Jammu & Kashmir, Remote Island Pincodes: 7 to 10 business days via India Post Speed Post or Air Cargo.

04. Shipping Charges & Free Express Delivery

Free Insured Express Courier: Syber Space provides 100% FREE express courier delivery on all confirmed hardware orders across India. There are zero hidden shipping, handling, or fuel surcharges added at checkout.

05. Authorized Courier Logistics Partners

To guarantee transit safety of sensitive optical and electronic surveillance hardware, we partner exclusively with verified national logistics carriers:

  • BlueDart Express: Primary air courier for high-value enterprise optical equipment.
  • Delhivery Surface & Express: Nationwide ground & express transit.
  • DTDC Courier: Extensive secondary district distribution.
  • India Post Speed Post: Government-certified delivery for remote non-standard pin codes.

06. Consignment Tracking & Notifications

Immediately upon courier pickup, an automated dispatch notification containing the Airway Bill (AWB) Tracking Number and direct courier tracking URL is delivered to the customer via SMS and registered Email address. Customers can monitor transit milestones in real time.

07. Damaged or Tampered Shipments Protocol

All hardware shipments are packed in heavy-duty tamper-evident reinforced boxes with Syber Space security tape. If the outer carton shows evident signs of tampering, slicing, or physical damage at the time of delivery:

  • Do not accept the delivery or sign the courier slip with the remark "Package Received Damaged".
  • Record a brief video or clear photograph of the exterior packaging.
  • Contact our emergency helpline at +91 75111 80808 or email info@syberspace.in within 24 hours. We will coordinate immediate replacement dispatch.
