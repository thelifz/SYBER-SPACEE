Cancellation, Returns & Refund Policy

Entity: Syber Space Security Systems Standard Refund Timeline: 5 to 7 Working Days Return Window: 7 Calendar Days

01. Overview & Customer Commitment

At Syber Space Security Systems, our priority is providing flawless surveillance security equipment to our commercial installers, enterprise partners, and retail buyers. We adhere strictly to the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, and the merchant guidelines established by HDFC Bank SmartGateway™.

This comprehensive policy transparently details the conditions, procedures, and timelines under which orders may be cancelled, returned, replaced, or refunded.

02. Order Cancellation Policy

Cancellation Window: Orders can be cancelled free of charge at any time prior to courier handover and dispatch (typically within 24 to 48 hours of order placement, prior to courier handover).
  • How to Cancel: To cancel an order, immediately contact our dispatch desk at +91 75111 80808 or email info@syberspace.in with your Order Reference Number (e.g. SS-HDFC-XXXXXX).
  • Post-Dispatch Cancellation: If an order has already been handed over to our express logistics courier partner and airway bill (AWB) tracking has been generated, the order cannot be cancelled in transit. The customer must receive the parcel and follow the standard Return Procedure.
  • Cancellation by Syber Space: Syber Space reserves the right to cancel an order in rare instances such as unforeseen inventory depletion, errors in technical specifications/pricing, or flagged fraudulent payment activity by HDFC Bank risk engines. In such cases, 100% of the paid amount is immediately reversed.

03. 7-Day Return & Replacement Policy

We offer a 7-Day Replacement Guarantee from the date of confirmed courier delivery if any of the following conditions occur:

  • Defective or Dead-on-Arrival (DOA) Hardware: The camera, PoE switch, router, or NVR does not power on or exhibits functional optical/electronic defect upon initial setup.
  • Transit Damage: The physical parcel or product enclosure was physically damaged or cracked during courier transit.
  • Incorrect Hardware Received: The model, resolution, or variant delivered does not match the item specified on your procurement tax invoice.
Eligibility Conditions for Return:
  • The return request must be raised within 7 calendar days of receiving delivery.
  • Hardware must be in its original physical condition, without signs of tampering, burnt circuitry, or unauthorized repair attempts.
  • The unit must be returned with all original manufacturer packaging, user manuals, accessories, power adapters, and mounting screws included.

04. Return Inspection & Reverse Pickup

Upon receiving your return request with unboxing photos/video documentation, our engineering team will initiate a reverse courier pickup from your address within 24 to 48 hours. Once received at our Kerala hub, our technical staff will inspect the hardware within 2 working days. If verified, an expedited brand-new replacement unit will be dispatched, or a full refund will be initiated as requested.

05. Refund Turnaround Timeline & Mode of Payment (HDFC Mandate)

Statutory Refund Turnaround: All approved refunds are processed and credited back to the customer's original payment method within 5 to 7 working days (excluding bank holidays and Sundays).

In strict accordance with HDFC Bank acquiring rules and RBI payment protocols, refunds are credited exclusively via the original payment rail:

  • UPI Transactions: Credited directly back to the original Virtual Payment Address (VPA) / linked bank account within 24 to 48 banking hours.
  • Credit / Debit Cards: Initiated via HDFC Bank SmartGateway™ to the issuing card company within 48 hours; reflecting on your bank card statement within 5 to 7 business days depending on the card issuer bank.
  • NetBanking: Credited back to the originating Indian bank account within 3 to 5 banking days.
  • EasyEMI Purchases: Cancellation of EMI schedule and credit reversal automated in coordination with HDFC Bank card operations.

Note: Syber Space will NEVER issue refunds in cash, via third-party unrelated accounts, or through unsecured channels.

06. Non-Returnable Items

The following items are non-returnable unless received in a damaged or non-functional state:

  • Custom-cut surveillance networking cable drums that have been sliced or partially installed.
  • Hardware with altered, defaced, or removed serial number barcode stickers.
  • Damage resulting from lightning strikes, electrical surges due to ungrounded wiring, or water immersion exceeding IP ratings.
